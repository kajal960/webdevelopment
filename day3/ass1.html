About our Cozy Cafe

Cuddle Cup Cafe & Tea is a highly praised boba tea shop known for its delicious and refreshing drinks, including specialty milk teas and unique flavors like taro cheese foam and coconut slush. The store offers a warm, cozy, and aesthetically pleasing atmosphere with friendly and attentive customer service that makes patrons feel welcomed and valued. The clean and stylish interior, combined with a varied menu and customizable options, contributes to an enjoyable experience. Customers consistently recommend this spot for its quality beverages and hospitable environment.Welcome to our cozy Bobatea and Coffee shop where we believe in delivering above and beyond customer service. We offer amazing Boba Tea drinks, as well as specialty coffee, along with treats and specialty house-made desserts such as egg wallfes, milk-tea favorites creme brulee, Dubai chocolate and ice cream, together with a savory treats special called Vietnamese Hotdog.
